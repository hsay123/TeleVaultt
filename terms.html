Independent project

TeleVault is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram. You supply your own Telegram account and API credentials, and you are responsible for following Telegram's Terms of Service when you use the app.

Your files and your key

File contents are encrypted on your device when encryption is on, but file names, paths, sizes and folder names are not encrypted. The encryption key stays in your operating system keychain. If you lose it, encrypted files cannot be read. This beta has no recovery-key export, so keep a backup of your keychain. More detail is on the privacy page.

Deletion and sync

“Delete permanently” is irreversible: it deletes the file's recorded Telegram messages and its local records. Cached thumbnails, old sync snapshots and archived version messages are not removed. Trash expires after 30 days with a local-only purge, so those messages stay in your Telegram account. Sync is last-writer-wins, and a local backup is made before a pull is applied.

Updates

The startup update check contacts GitHub and sends a random local ID. A failed check is silent. An installer is downloaded only after you choose to download it. Installers and verification steps are on the download page.
